# Balance adjuster assignment by open workload

**Assigned:** Priya Raman

Assignment is round-robin over the whole team, which means an adjuster sitting
on nine open total-loss files gets the tenth. Severity, not count, is what
fills a day.

- Weight the queue by open claim severity, not open claim count.
- Respect licence by state and line before anything else.
- Leave a manual override, logged, for the cases the rule gets wrong
  (claims/auto-accidents/assign-adjuster.feature.md).
