# Tell claimants what is happening without them asking

**Assigned:** Priya Raman

Most inbound claim calls are status checks. They are cheap to answer and
expensive to receive.

- Notify on the milestones that actually change: adjuster assigned, inspection
  scheduled, estimate written, payment issued.
- Channel per the insured's stated preference, honoured.
- Measure the call volume the notifications displace
  (claims/auto-accidents/assign-adjuster.feature.md).
