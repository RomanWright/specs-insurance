# Decide what backdating a cancellation is allowed to do

**Assigned:** Dana Whitfield

Cancellations arrive late — the insured sold the car three weeks ago and tells
us now. Underwriting backdates, billing refunds from a different date, and the
two disagree by a full billing cycle.

- One rule for how far back a cancellation may be dated, by reason.
- Proof required for anything beyond that window.
- The refund calculation reads the same effective date the cancellation does
  (policy/auto-policy/cancel-policy.feature.md,
  billing/refund-unearned-premium.feature.md).
