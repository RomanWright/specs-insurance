# Support lay-up periods on boat policies

**Assigned:** Jonas Berg

Northern boats are out of the water five months a year and we charge for
twelve. Competitors do not, and our retention in those states shows it.

- Lay-up dates on the policy, with a reduced-coverage period.
- Premium credit computed from the lay-up months, not applied as a flat
  discount.
- Claims during lay-up checked against the reduced coverage
  (policy/boat-policy/quote-boat-policy.feature.md).
