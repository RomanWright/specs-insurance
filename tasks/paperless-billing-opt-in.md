# Make paperless billing an explicit opt-in with a paper trail

**Assigned:** Miguel Santos

We flipped a fair number of accounts to paperless during the portal launch and
cannot now prove any of them consented. That is a compliance problem before it
is a UX one.

- Consent captured with timestamp, channel, and the exact wording shown.
- One-click return to paper, honoured on the next cycle.
- Notices that must be mailed by statute stay mailed regardless
  (billing/image-based-billing.feature.md).
