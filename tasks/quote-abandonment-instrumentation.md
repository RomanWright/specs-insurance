# Instrument where quotes get abandoned

**Assigned:** Priya Raman

Roughly half of started auto quotes never reach a price and nobody can say at
which question they stop. Everything we might do about it is a guess until
this exists.

- Event per question answered, per quote, with time on step.
- Funnel by line and by channel.
- Separate abandonment from decline — they look identical in the current data
  (policy/auto-policy/quote-policy.feature.md).
