# Let an insured change payment plan mid-term

**Assigned:** Jonas Berg

Moving from monthly to pay-in-full today means a cancel and rewrite, which
resets the term and loses the renewal discount. Everyone involved knows it is
absurd.

- Plan change as an endorsement, keeping the term intact.
- Re-spread the remaining balance across the new schedule.
- Installment fees adjusted, not refunded and re-charged
  (billing/collect-premium.feature.md).
