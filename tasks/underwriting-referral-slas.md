# Put a clock on underwriting referrals

**Assigned:** Miguel Santos

A referred quote sits in a shared mailbox until someone opens it. Median is
under a day, the tail is a week, and the tail is where the bound business goes
elsewhere.

- Referrals in a queue with an owner, not a mailbox.
- Target response time by referral reason, measured.
- Escalation when a referral ages past its target
  (policy/auto-policy/bind-policy.feature.md).
