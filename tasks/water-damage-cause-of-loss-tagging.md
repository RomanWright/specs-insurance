# Tag water claims by actual cause of loss

**Assigned:** Jonas Berg

Every water claim lands in one bucket. Burst pipe, appliance failure, roof
leak, and sewer backup have nothing in common except the water, and the
climate work upstairs needs them apart.

- A required cause-of-loss code at first notice of loss.
- Backfill the last two years from adjuster notes where it can be inferred,
  and mark what could not.
- Feed the codes to underwriting aggregation
  (claims/home-claims/record-water-damage-claim.feature.md).
