# Pilot photo estimates for small collision claims

**Assigned:** Aisha Bello

Under a few thousand dollars, a physical inspection costs more in cycle time
than it saves in accuracy. Run a limited pilot before anyone proposes it as
policy.

- Damage photos from the insured, with guided capture prompts.
- Estimate written from photos for claims under the pilot threshold.
- Measure re-inspection rate and supplement rate against the control group
  (claims/auto-accidents/settle-collision-claim.feature.md).
