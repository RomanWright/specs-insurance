# Refund to the method that paid

**Assigned:** Aisha Bello

Every refund goes out as a cheque, including for the insured who paid by card
last week. Cheques take days, get lost, and cost more than the refund
sometimes is.

- Refund to the original method where it is still valid.
- Cheque as the fallback, with the reason recorded.
- Escheatment handling for the ones nobody cashes
  (billing/refund-unearned-premium.feature.md).
