# Write the roof age rule down

**Assigned:** Dana Whitfield

**Status:** Done

Underwriters decline old roofs on homeowners quotes, and each of them has a
slightly different age in mind. The rule exists; it just lives in five heads.

- One published threshold by roof material and state.
- Inspection or replacement documentation as the exception path.
- Applied at quote, not discovered at inspection after the customer has
  committed (policy/home-policy/quote-homeowners-policy.feature.md).
